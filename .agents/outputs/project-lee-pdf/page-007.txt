7. Portability 
 
LEE must remain portable from the beginning. 
 
The Brain should be: 
 
- exportable 
- versioned 
- restorable 
- migratable 
- auditable 
- independently verifiable 
 
The K6 is intended to become LEE's primary local home. 
 
It should not become a permanent architectural dependency. 
 
The principle is: 
 
«Move Lee, not rebuild Lee.»
