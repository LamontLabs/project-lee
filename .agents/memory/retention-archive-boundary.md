---
name: Retention archive boundary
description: Content-addressed source preservation, layered media lineage, pressure response, and owner-controlled archive changes
---

Archive placement is an auditable projection of source evidence, not a replacement for the PostgreSQL Brain. Original media, transcripts, structured facts, commitments, semantic entries, and summaries remain separate representations with provenance; checksums determine integrity, while retention temperature and importance/protection remain independent.

**Why:** Storage pressure must reduce cost and active footprint without silently forgetting owner history, canonical evidence, constitutional knowledge, or audit records.

**How to apply:** Resolve source-specific retention policy before archiving, preserve content-addressed manifests and restore references in backups, assess pressure through staged dry-run actions, and require explicit owner decisions before archive movement or purge. Recovery and safe modes block background retention writes just like HTTP mutations.
