60. Least Authority 
 
Permissions should exist at the capability level. 
 
For example: 
 
Greyline may permit LEE to: 
 
- inspect queue 
- begin approved production 
- pause 
- retry validation 
 
while: 
 
- publish externally 
 
requires CerbaSeal and human confirmation. 
 
The system should automate safely without collapsing all authority into one master credential.
