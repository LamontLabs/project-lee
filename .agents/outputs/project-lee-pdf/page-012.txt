14. Assumptions 
 
Unverified beliefs should be explicitly tracked as Assumptions. 
 
Possible lifecycle: 
 
- active 
- under review 
- invalidated 
- archived 
 
When an assumption becomes invalid, LEE should know what conclusions depended on it.
