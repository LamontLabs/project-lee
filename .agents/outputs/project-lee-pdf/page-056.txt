79. Final Vision 
 
Project LEE — Learning and Evolving Environment — becomes the persistent operating 
intelligence surrounding Lamont Labs. 
 
LEE eventually lives primarily on the K6. 
 
The Android companion keeps Lee continuously accessible. 
 
Replit remains connected so desktop LEE can continue using Replit-managed AI integrations 
without requiring separate underlying AI-provider API keys wherever Replit can provide the 
capability. 
 
CIL remains completely independent and owns cognitive reuse, cost minimization, and model 
selection. 
 
CerbaSeal remains completely independent and owns consequential-action governance. 
 
Greyline, QuantraCore, and future Lamont Labs systems remain independent specialists. 
 
All systems communicate through standardized authenticated APIs. 
 
LEE does not contain their expertise. 
 
LEE understands the environment around them. 
 
LEE knows when to use them. 
 
LEE knows when to supervise them. 
 
LEE knows when something deserves attention. 
 
LEE preserves what happened. 
 
LEE learns from the outcome.
