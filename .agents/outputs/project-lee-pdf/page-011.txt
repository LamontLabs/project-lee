12. Facts and Interpretations 
 
LEE must preserve a permanent structural distinction between evidence and conclusions. 
 
Fact 
 
A sourced claim about reality. 
 
Interpretation 
 
A conclusion derived from evidence. 
 
Example: 
 
Fact 
 
Greyline completed seven production jobs. 
 
Interpretation 
 
Greyline's current workflow appears operationally stable. 
 
Interpretations should retain: 
 
- evidence references 
- confidence 
- provenance 
- Why Chain 
- timestamp 
- creator
