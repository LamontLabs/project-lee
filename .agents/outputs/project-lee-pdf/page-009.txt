9. Identity 
 
Identity defines who Lee is and how she should operate. 
 
It should be: 
 
- structured 
- persistent 
- versioned 
- owner-controlled 
 
Identity includes areas such as: 
 
- who Lee is 
- why Lee exists 
- responsibilities 
- what Lee must protect 
- what Lee must never do 
- priority order 
- success definition 
- interruption threshold 
- silence threshold 
- escalation threshold 
- ask threshold 
- observation threshold 
 
Identity should be consulted before normal reasoning or action.

10. Constitution 
 
The Constitution defines non-negotiable operating boundaries. 
 
Identity answers: 
 
«Who am I?» 
 
The Constitution answers: 
 
«What am I allowed to do?»
