---
name: Institutional knowledge promotion
description: Promotion rules for turning operational outcomes into durable institutional knowledge.
---

Institutional knowledge requires three independent supporting outcomes, a stable provenance chain, and no unresolved contradiction in the same pattern group. Contradictions force review rather than promotion; owner decisions remain explicit and auditable.

**Why:** Repeated event types alone can overfit one aggregate or silently convert an exception into a durable organizational belief.

**How to apply:** Count distinct aggregate-backed outcomes, retain Experience and Lesson references, emit Pattern and review evidence, and persist defer, reject, invalidation, and supersession transitions.
