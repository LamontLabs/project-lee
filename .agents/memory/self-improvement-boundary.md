---
name: Self-improvement boundary
description: The durable contract for bounded, reversible operational adaptation.
---

Self-improvement may change only an explicit allowlist of output-behavior parameters after sufficient evidence. Protected identity, Constitution, facts, knowledge, anchors, CerbaSeal governance, owner permissions, and credentials are rejected explicitly.

**Why:** Adaptation is useful only when it cannot silently rewrite the system’s authority, truth, ownership, or safety boundaries.

**How to apply:** Persist evidence references, observation count, reason, prior/new values, timestamp, and rollback data; emit applied/rejected events; keep owner reset and disabled states durable.
