75. Permanent Architectural Invariants 
 
The following rules should be considered foundational: 
 
«CIL and CerbaSeal are independent Lamont Labs systems, not internal LEE engines.» 
 
«LEE communicates with CIL and CerbaSeal only through authenticated APIs.» 
 
«LEE may contain integration clients and contracts but never duplicate their specialist decision 
logic.» 
 
«CIL is the authoritative cognitive-routing and model-selection system.» 
 
«No normal LEE-originated paid/external model call occurs before CIL evaluates the request.» 
 
«The Model Router executes CIL's decision rather than replacing it.» 
 
«The Replit AI Bridge executes approved model requests but does not independently choose or 
substitute models.»
