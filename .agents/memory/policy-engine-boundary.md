---
name: Policy Engine boundary
description: Mutable, versioned operational rules sit between Constitution and case-by-case Governance.
---

Policies are standing operational rules, not constitutional truths or individual approvals: active versions are consulted and audited, while blocked actions create Governance HOLDs that may be overridden without mutating the Constitution.

**Why:** Cost, privacy, retention, notification, relationship, backup, and connector behavior must evolve consistently without constitutional amendments or repeated manual decisions.

**How to apply:** Keep policy values versioned with prior records retained, return the applied version and constraints from every check, and log both permitted consultations and violations.
