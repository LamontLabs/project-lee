---
name: Commitment waiting boundary
description: Evidence-backed commitment extraction and relationship-aware waiting severity
---

Commitments remain conservative, provider-neutral records: explicit promises and clear waiting language may surface, while ambiguity alone must not create a waiting loop. When a person’s established communication cadence is known, it is a valid severity input even if the commitment has no explicit cadence.

**Why:** A useful relationship view needs to distinguish a genuinely overdue obligation from normal communication rhythm without inventing promises or triggering automatic follow-up.

**How to apply:** Preserve source and completion evidence on commitment lifecycle changes; use normalized person/interaction cadence as a scoring input, never as permission to contact someone or mutate an external provider.
