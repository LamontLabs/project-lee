59. Security 
 
System-to-system APIs should support strong authentication. 
 
Depending on deployment, mechanisms may include: 
 
- TLS 
- scoped service tokens 
- HMAC signing 
- timestamps 
- nonce/replay protection 
- short-lived credentials 
- device identity 
- network restrictions 
- capability-scoped permissions 
 
LEE should never gain unrestricted authority simply because a system is connected.
