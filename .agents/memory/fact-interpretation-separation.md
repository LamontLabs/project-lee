---
name: Fact and interpretation separation
description: Rules for keeping source-backed world statements distinct from Lee-generated reasoning.
---

Facts require source evidence and use observed, extracted, declared, or verified types. Interpretations require linked facts and use reasoning types such as patterns, predictions, opportunities, strategies, and simulations.

**Why:** Mixing a statement about what happened with a conclusion about what it means makes confidence, canon promotion, and provenance impossible to audit safely.

**How to apply:** Keep ledger APIs, context labels, UI badges, and promotion flows distinct. Internal ledger writes must not be mistaken for external side effects by Constitution security heuristics.
