Project LEE 
 
Learning and Evolving Environment 
 
Persistent Founder Operating Environment and Lamont Labs Orchestration Layer 
 
--- 
 
1. Core Definition 
 
Project LEE — Learning and Evolving Environment — is the persistent operating intelligence 
layer for Lamont Labs. 
 
LEE is designed to understand the full operational picture across projects, people, systems, 
services, objectives, decisions, events, relationships, dependencies, risks, costs, and 
accumulated experience. 
 
LEE is not intended to perform every specialized task herself. 
 
Instead: 
 
«LEE understands, coordinates, supervises, remembers, prioritizes, and orchestrates. 
Specialized systems perform their own specialist work. APIs connect them.» 
 
LEE therefore sits above the broader Lamont Labs ecosystem. 
 
Her job is to continuously answer questions such as: 
 
- What is happening? 
- What changed? 
- What deserves attention? 
- What am I waiting on? 
- What is blocked? 
- Which project is moving? 
- Which project is stagnating? 
- Which systems are healthy? 
- What can continue without intervention? 
- What requires approval? 
- What system should handle this? 
- What AI reasoning is actually required? 
- What did this cost? 
- What happened as a result? 
- What have we learned from similar outcomes?
